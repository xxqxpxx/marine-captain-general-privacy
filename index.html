Privacy policy: Marine Captain General V2 watch face and companion app

Last updated: 23 October 2026.

This policy covers the Marine Captain General V2 watch face (com.ahmed.watchface.marinenavigator.wff.play) and its optional companion app for Wear OS and Android phones (com.ahmed.watchface.marinenavigator), published by TA7 Software, LLC ("we").

Summary

  • The watch face contains no code. It does not collect, store or send any data. It only displays information that your watch, or complication apps you choose, provide to it.
  • The companion app works on your devices. We do not run servers that receive your personal data, we do not have user accounts, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • The only data that reaches us is crash reports, with personal data removed. They are optional: turn off "Send crash reports" in the app and nothing is sent.

Third-party services the app contacts

Each request carries only what is needed (for example rounded coordinates or a ticker symbol), over HTTPS:

  • Open-Meteo (weather, marine, air quality, geocoding) — https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
  • NOAA CO-OPS tides and currents (United States stations) — https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/
  • CoinGecko (crypto prices) — https://www.coingecko.com/en/privacy
  • Frankfurter (European Central Bank currency rates) — https://www.frankfurter.app/
  • The exchanges whose keys you add (MEXC, Binance, OKX, Bybit, Bitget), under their own privacy policies
  • Sentry (crash reports, see above) — https://sentry.io/privacy/

These services may see your IP address as part of a normal internet request. We have no control over their logs; see their policies.

Permissions

Location (approximate), physical activity and body sensors / heart rate (Health complication only), notifications (alerts, chime), exact alarms (hourly chime), vibration, internet and network state, run at startup (to re-arm the chime), foreground service (regatta start timer). Each is optional: features that need a permission you deny show "--" instead.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke any permission, delete exchange keys, turn off "Send crash reports", clear the app's storage or uninstall at any time; all locally stored data is then removed. To ask about or delete crash reports linked to your installation, email ahmedafatah95@gmail.com with the approximate date and device model. If you are in the EEA/UK you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection under the GDPR, and may complain to your data protection authority.

Not for navigation

Compass, tide, wind and weather information is for general information only and must not be used for navigation or safety-of-life decisions.
